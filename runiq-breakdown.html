The problem

Every bad offer costs you
real time and real money.

The math is instant and invisible

An offer shows up. You have seconds to decide. Doing the gas-versus-miles-versus-time math in your head while in a parking lot is how you take bad offers.

Deadhead miles kill your rate

The miles to pick up and the miles back to your zone aren't in the offer. But they eat your gas and your time — and they're the difference between a good run and a break-even one.

Gas cost is invisible until it isn't

At $3.60/gal and 24.5 MPG, every mile costs you money. Offers don't show you that. RunIQ does — before you tap accept.

No visibility on your actual day

Without tracking, you don't know what you've really made, what you've spent on gas, or whether your shift was actually worth it.
