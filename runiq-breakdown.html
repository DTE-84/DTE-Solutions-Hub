The Problem

Every mispriced offer costs you time, fuel, and margin.

Delivery apps highlight headline payout while leaving out the operating costs that decide whether a run actually pays.

01

Split-Second Mental Math

When an offer pings, you have seconds to respond. Estimating fuel burn, round-trip mileage, and effective hourly rate in your head under a timer leads to unprofitable accepts.

02

Unpaid Deadhead Miles

The drive back to your pickup zone never appears on the offer card. Those unpaid return miles consume both fuel and shift time—quietly turning a decent-looking run into a break-even trip.

03

Hidden Fuel Drag

At 24.5 MPG and $3.60/gal, every 15-mile round trip takes over $2.20 straight out of your pocket. Platforms show gross pay; RunIQ shows net take-home before you commit.

04

No Shift-Level Visibility

Without a clean ledger of accepted offers, tips, and estimated gas spend, it is impossible to tell which zones, hours, and order types actually generate your best return.
