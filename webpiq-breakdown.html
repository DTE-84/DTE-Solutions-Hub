The Bottleneck

Why generic image converters fail real sellers.

Squoosh and TinyPNG handle one image or a tiny batch with zero store presets—and cloud tools make you upload gigabytes of private photos to a remote server.

The 200-Photo Reseller Backlog

Shooting 40 items on an iPhone leaves you with 200+ HEIC/JPG files named IMG_4821. Cropping and renaming them one by one kills listing velocity.

Hidden Home GPS Metadata

Phone photos embed exact home latitude and longitude in EXIF headers. WEBPIQ re-encodes every frame clean so your home address never rides along in listing photos.

4 MB Photos Kill Storefront Speed

Uncompressed phone photos cripple Shopify and WooCommerce mobile load times. Converting to WebP cuts payload size by ~90% with no visible quality loss.

Zero-Upload Local Execution

Powered by multi-threaded Web Workers and WebAssembly SIMD. Process 500+ photos at once without freezing your tab or waiting on cloud uploads.
