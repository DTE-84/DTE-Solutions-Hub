The Bottleneck

Why generic image converters fail real sellers.

Single-image tools stall on real inventory volume, lack marketplace sizing presets, and cloud converters force you to upload gigabytes of private product shoots to third-party servers.

01

The 200-Photo Listing Backlog

Shooting 40 items on an iPhone leaves you with 200+ raw HEIC and JPG files named IMG_4821. Cropping, padding, and renaming them individually destroys listing velocity.

02

Hidden Home GPS Metadata

Phone photos embed exact home latitude, longitude, and camera serial headers in EXIF tags. WebPIQ re-encodes every frame from raw pixels so your home location never ships with a listing.

03

Bloated 4 MB Photos Slow Down Stores

Uncompressed camera uploads drag down Shopify and WooCommerce mobile load times. Converting to tuned WebP cuts payload sizes by ~90% with crisp visual clarity.

04

Cloud Upload Limits & Privacy Risk

Web converters throttle batch sizes to 20 files and send unreleased inventory to remote servers. WebPIQ runs 100% locally—fast, private, and functional even offline.
