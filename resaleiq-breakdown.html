The Problem

Reselling without intelligence is just gambling.

Most resellers price by instinct, list on the wrong marketplace, underestimate platform fees, and lose visibility into the capital tied up in their own inventory.

01

Manual Pricing Eats Your Day

Combing through sold comps across three or four platforms takes 15–20 minutes per item. At 20 items a day, hours of research stall listings that should go live in seconds.

02

Wrong Platform, Wrong Buyer

A pair of Nike Dunks commands completely different demand on StockX, eBay, Vinted, and Facebook Marketplace. Listing in the wrong channel leaves money sitting unsold.

03

Platform Fees Erode Margin Silently

Every marketplace takes a different cut. Overlooking Poshmark's 20% fee or combined payment and shipping deductions can turn a $40 projected flip into $12 overnight.

04

Invisible Inventory Capital

Without structured portfolio tracking, your working capital stays trapped in unlisted or stale stock—with no real-time read on active yield, sell-through, or gross revenue.
