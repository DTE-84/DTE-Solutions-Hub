The Problem

Subscription chaos costs you more than money.

Recurring charges stack quietly across multiple cards while passwords scatter across browsers and old notes—leaving you blind the moment a card expires or gets compromised.

"My card just got compromised."

You need to swap payment methods across every recurring bill—but you don't know which services are tied to that card. That turns a routine replacement into a 45-minute bank-statement hunt.

"Is Hulu on the Visa or Chase checking? Was that gym membership auto-renewed? What else is tied to it?"

"Wait—I'm still paying for that?"

A streaming plan you stopped watching months ago. A free trial that quietly became $19.99/month. A membership from a city you moved away from. Subscriptions are engineered to stay invisible.

"I just spotted three recurring charges I didn't recognize—I've been paying them for nearly a year."

"What was my password again?"

You try to cancel a service and hit a login wall. The password lives in an old browser or buried note, and the reset email routes to an old inbox—turning a 2-minute cancel into a stalled chore.

"I spent 20 minutes trying to cancel one subscription just because I couldn't get into the account."
