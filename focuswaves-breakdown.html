01 · The ADHD Friction Problem

Generic playlists weren't built for neurodivergent executive function.

ADHD focus rarely breaks because of a lack of willpower—it breaks at task initiation, sudden sensory interruptions, and mid-session context switching.

Task Initiation Paralysis

Staring at a blank screen with 12 tabs open drains working memory before minute one. Heavy menus, logins, and playlist hunting become another barrier to starting.

"I spent 20 minutes picking a focus playlist and forgot what I actually sat down to build."

Sensory Spike Derailment

Pure silence amplifies every hallway footstep, notification buzz, and HVAC click—while raw sine waves alone become fatiguing after ten minutes.

"One sudden sound in the next room pulls me completely out of deep work."

The Mid-Session Thought Spiral

A stray reminder pops up mid-sprint. Switching tabs to handle it "for 30 seconds" turns into a 40-minute derailment and an unfinished work block.

"If I don't write it down immediately I'll lose it—but leaving my workspace kills my momentum."
02 · System Architecture

Four engineered layers in one distraction-free cockpit.

FocusWaves pairs real-time acoustic synthesis with practical ADHD executive-function guardrails so users can lock in with a single click.

Layer 01

Dual-Mode Web Audio DSP Engine

Generates real-time stereo Binaural Beats (baseFreq ± beatFreq/2 hard-panned L/R for headphones) and speaker-safe Isochronic pulses (LFO amplitude-modulated carrier) with smooth 350ms fade envelopes—zero pops, zero static MP3 loops.

60fps AnalyserNode Canvas Oscilloscope
Layer 02

ADHD Colored Noise Sensory Masking

Synthesizes seamless looped Brown (Brownian), Pink (1/f), and White noise buffers passed through tailored biquad low-pass filters. Independent Noise Layer Mix control lets listeners wrap crisp carriers in warm acoustic cushioning.

Real-Time Low-Pass Filtered Buffers
Layer 03

Executive Function Guardrails

Includes a Single-Task Anchor input ("What is your ONE objective?"), 1-tap quick-launch ADHD presets (15m Initiation, 45m Deep Focus, Sensory Reset), a real countdown timer with harmonic completion chime, and a 2-second Brain-Dump Parking Lot.

Full Distraction-Free Focus Mode
Layer 04

Deterministic Local Telemetry & Media Controls

Tracks focused minutes, completed blocks, active daily streaks, and dominant wave band inside client-side localStorage. Registers native MediaSession handlers so keyboard keys and mobile lock screens control playback seamlessly.

Zero Accounts · Zero Tracking
